# Demo and UAT Walkthrough

Use demo accounts only when `SEED_DEMO_DATA=true`. Password: `Demo123!` unless changed.

1. Sign in as Operations Manager and create a sampling order.
2. Add an assignment with source `TSCO` or `CLIENT`.
3. Sign in as Sampling Supervisor and assign the sampler.
4. Sign in through Field Sampling and complete GPS/photo/sample data.
5. Sign in as Data Entry and Accept/Register in mock LMS.
6. Confirm the sample shows `Awaiting Physical Receipt` and a LMS number.
7. Sign in as Receiving, scan/search the barcode and confirm receipt.
8. Sign in as Laboratory Chemist and open Laboratory Progress, Workstations, Retained Samples and Quality Control from the unified sidebar.
9. Enter sample results and move the batch to Technical Review.
10. Sign in as Senior Chemist; review Technical Supervision and Technical Assessment.
11. Approve the technical comment/report workflow.
12. Sign in as Client and confirm only the client company sample and external milestones are visible.
13. Sign in as Management and review sample status and sampler/error metrics.
14. Sign in as Administrator and verify companies/users/assets/commercial data/audit pages.
