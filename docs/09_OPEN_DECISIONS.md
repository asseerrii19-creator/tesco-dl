# Decisions That Cannot Be Guessed

The foundation deliberately leaves these configurable or mocked until official information is supplied:

1. Current LMS product/version and interface.
2. Who owns the official LMS sample number.
3. Barcode format and label size approved by Operations.
4. Whether one sample request can have multiple containers and separate barcodes.
5. Quotation/PO master source: ERP, LMS, manual or API.
6. Corporate identity provider and SSO rules.
7. Client portal authentication and invitation process.
8. Status wording visible to clients.
9. SLA targets and estimated completion logic.
10. File retention and photo privacy rules.
11. Geographic privacy and consent for employee tracking.
12. Official data fields required by each client.
13. Offline operation requirements and maximum offline duration.
