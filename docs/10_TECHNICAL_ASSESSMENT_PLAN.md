# Technical Assessment — v1.2 Pilot

## Implemented

The platform reads the latest results from Full Laboratory Operations and provides an internal reviewer-support page for:

- selected IEC 60422:2024 transformer-oil screening bands (initial category-A style profile);
- water/BDV correlation reminder;
- IFT with complementary acidity/DDF follow-up reminder;
- IEC 60599:2022 gas-ratio screening for PD, D1, D2, T1, T2 and T3 patterns;
- CO2/CO display when data is available;
- reviewer decision: Draft, Retest, Resample, Escalate or Approve;
- audit and chain-of-custody events.

## Control statement

The output is **system-assisted internal screening only**. It is not automatically shown to the client and does not independently determine transformer condition. The Senior Chemist/Quality reviewer owns the recommendation.

## Required future profiles

- full IEC 60422 equipment categories and asset-specific limits;
- IEEE C57.104 DGA status and multi-point rate/trend analysis;
- IEEE C57.106 new/in-service/reconditioned oil profiles by voltage class;
- client specifications and manufacturer limits with explicit precedence;
- versioned Quality approval and effective dates.

The copyrighted standards are not republished as public files in this package.
